Ramón Sánchez Iborra from Universidad de Murcia participated in SECYT'26 (Semana de la Ciencia y la Tecnología de Murcia) held in Murcia from 23 to 25 October, presenting the work and results of the ONOFRE-4 project.

The event aimed to bring scientific knowledge closer to society, promote scientific and technological culture, and encourage public participation in science.


More details: https://fseneca.es/secyt26/
