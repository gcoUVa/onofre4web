On 25 September 2026, the team from Universidad de Murcia participated in the European Researchers' Night in Murcia. They set up a stand focused on the latest advances in communication networks and artificial intelligence, showcasing interactive demonstrations of AI and future network technologies, as well as research projects developed by the group. 
Visitors could take part in an educational arcade-style game about future networks and AI, see a real-time demonstration of AI in action, and discover how current and emerging network technologies work. These activities allowed families and non-expert audiences of all ages to explore key concepts related to future networks and artificial intelligence in an accessible and engaging way.


More details: https://www.um.es/web/ucc/proyectos/la-noche-de-los-investigadores
